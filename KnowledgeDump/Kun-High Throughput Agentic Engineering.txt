High Throughput Agentic Engineering with Kun  |  https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MSbacZ99E14  |  09/13/2026

---

Kun Chen demonstrates an advanced **agentic engineering workflow** that enables a single developer to manage dozens of projects simultaneously through **high-throughput multitasking**. At the center of this ecosystem is **First Mate**, an open-source orchestrator that serves as a primary interface, delegating complex technical tasks to specialized **crew mates** based on real-time **LLM quota availability** and specific model strengths. The process is supported by custom tools like **Herder** for session multiplexing and **Lavish** for visual prototyping, allowing the user to provide high-level feedback on interactive artifacts rather than monitoring raw code. Ultimately, Chen emphasizes that by automating routine coordination and **optimizing human attention**, engineers can shift their focus from low-level implementation details to the more valuable work of **strategic product design**.
