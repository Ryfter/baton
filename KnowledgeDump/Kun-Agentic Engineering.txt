---

In this video walkthrough, former principal engineer Kun Chen outlines a sophisticated **agentic engineering workflow** designed to boost productivity through high-level delegation. He advocates for a **terminal-centric environment** using tools like WezTerm, tmux, and Neovim to maintain a distraction-free "flow state" that is easily portable across devices. The core philosophy treats AI agents as **onboarded crewmates** whose performance is optimized through **structured memory files** and specialized skills rather than generic internet scripts. To manage complexity, Chen introduces a suite of custom open-source tools: **Lavish** for visual planning, **No Mistakes** for automated quality assurance, and **First Mate** to orchestrate multiple parallel agent sessions. Ultimately, the workflow shifts the developer's role from a manual coder to a **strategic captain**, focusing on high-level direction while a fleet of agents handles the iterative execution.
