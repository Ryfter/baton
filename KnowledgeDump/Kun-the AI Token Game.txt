---

The provided source investigates the phenomenon of **token maxing**, a strategy where tech companies and engineers are incentivized to consume massive amounts of artificial intelligence data to inflate perceived productivity. The author reveals a **circular capital game** where tech giants like Microsoft and Google invest in AI startups to ensure their own cloud infrastructure is utilized, thereby creating an illusion of **rising revenue** that masks the actual costs of computation. This ecosystem generates a manufactured sense of **FOMO (fear of missing out)** among enterprise leaders, who are often the only ones paying full price for expensive API tokens while individuals and startups use subsidized versions. To navigate this landscape, the text advises leaders to prioritize **business outcomes** over usage metrics and encourages individuals to focus on **high-quality success stories** rather than the sheer volume of AI-generated content.
